## Conditions for replacement and readback

A replacement needs the layer owner's reviewed comparison amendment or complete newly dated comparison under the existing protocol, with cumulative unresolved candidates and stopped/completed sweeps preserved. Join the exact candidate pin to upstream E2E, real full-harness invocation, quality, usage and recovery evidence. Use prospective exact input materialization and a declared retention layout, then review the actual artifacts.

Authentic October 10 manifest/returns/corrected inputs and the source bodies behind #993/#995 remain unrecovered. Do not reconstruct them or infer clean saturation. These historical gaps do not prevent independent runtime work or justify inventing a private approval ledger.

After this record lands, a fresh native session must read back the exact landed disposition, source bindings and unmet inputs. The bounded detector→worker→review→retention loop is not declared complete until that separate readback exists. Neither this record nor that future readback qualifies the entire runtime foundation.

## Consequences and alternatives

This retains an honest negative outcome that later sessions can apply. It avoids silently replacing a catalog decision with a newer host installation. A future source-backed replacement explicitly supersedes this dated retention record. Re-running the broad audit, deleting its failed artifacts, assuming missing history, or labeling the layer FINAL would not resolve the gates and was rejected.
