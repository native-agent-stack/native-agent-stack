# Codex pin change: retain the catalog pending evidence gates

**Status:** accepted upon merge through the supported NAS lander. The native-clients layer remains recorded, not FINAL.

## Context and owner direction

The owner asked on 2026-10-18 for a small, independently complete and currently evidenced runtime foundation, with durable decisions, preserved failures and explicit supersessions that later sessions can revisit before North Star work.

The authentic currency detector found Codex 0.160.0 in the foundation-cpu catalog while the host ran 0.163.0. A full native GPT-6.1 Sol/ultra worker and populated native readiness Workflow completed. The review reported 155 claims checked, 150 confirmed and five corrected, and concluded `NOT_YET_EVIDENCE_COMPLETE`. These are the original review's counts, not an independently established gold oracle.

## Decision

Retain the existing 0.160.0 catalog record and existing W6 comparison pending their evidence gates. This decision does not downgrade the installed 0.163.0 client or change the owner-facing runtimes. An installed release, source audit, cryptographic custody check or completed review alone is not a qualified layer replacement.

The normalized [retention receipt](../../evidence/artifacts/runtime-compounding-codex-pin-retention-20261018/receipt.json) binds the authentic detector, native sessions, original negative result, private archive and separately dated later evidence. The original raw artifacts remain in owner-host custody; the committed receipt is an allowlisted derivative, not a claim that public summary bytes equal the raw archive.

The exact-materialization failure, distinct child-usage helper invocation, missing frozen fresh-CLI streams and retention-layout deviations remain recorded. Later archival or a prospective corrected run cannot turn the old run into acceptance.

One earlier finding requires correction: the claim that the E2E proposal was bound nowhere is refuted. Native request 909 and result 917 hashed the 15,647-byte proposal to `f31176fb28912468653363d795788d887e9f88753f1b6a81a45055054314fa09` before Workflow invocation 1024. A narrower mandatory-publication-graph violation remains unproven without the actual contract. The original reviewer output remains intact; it is not itself a gold oracle.

## Conditions for replacement and readback

A replacement needs the layer owner's reviewed comparison amendment or complete newly dated comparison under the existing protocol, with cumulative unresolved candidates and stopped/completed sweeps preserved. Join the exact candidate pin to upstream E2E, real full-harness invocation, quality, usage and recovery evidence. Use prospective exact input materialization and a declared retention layout, then review the actual artifacts.

Authentic October 10 manifest/returns/corrected inputs and the source bodies behind #993/#995 remain unrecovered. Do not reconstruct them or infer clean saturation. These historical gaps do not prevent independent runtime work or justify inventing a private approval ledger.

After this record lands, a fresh native session must read back the exact landed disposition, source bindings and unmet inputs. The bounded detector→worker→review→retention loop is not declared complete until that separate readback exists. Neither this record nor that future readback qualifies the entire runtime foundation.

## Consequences and alternatives

This retains an honest negative outcome that later sessions can apply. It avoids silently replacing a catalog decision with a newer host installation. A future source-backed replacement explicitly supersedes this dated retention record. Re-running the broad audit, deleting its failed artifacts, assuming missing history, or labeling the layer FINAL would not resolve the gates and was rejected.
